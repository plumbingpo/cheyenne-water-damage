Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Cheyenne or the surrounding area, Cheyenne Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Cheyenne

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength.
