Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Cheyenne

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
